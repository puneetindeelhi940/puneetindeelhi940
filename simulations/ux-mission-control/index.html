Methods & sources: the project data is simulated, the methods and thresholds are real
  • Double Diamond (Discover, Define, Develop, Deliver): UK Design Council (2005), as taught by Nielsen Norman Group. Mapped to the Stanford d.school modes: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test.
  • d.school Bootcamp Bootleg: empathy maps, extreme users, POV madlib, “How might we”, brainstorm rules, post-it voting, Wizard of Oz prototyping.
  • Saturation around 12 interviews: Guest, Bunce & Johnson (2006).
  • Problems found = 1 − (1 − L)n, L ≈ 0.31: Nielsen & Landauer (1993). NN/g recommends about 40 participants for quantitative usability studies.
  • Heuristic evaluation with Nielsen’s 10 heuristics and 0–4 severity ratings.
  • SUS average ≈ 68, curved grading scale (Sauro & Lewis). SEQ task average ≈ 5.5 (Sauro). Adjusted-Wald confidence intervals for completion rates.
  • HEART framework: Rodden, Hutchinson & Fu, Google (2010). RICE prioritisation: Intercom.
  • A/B sample size from a two-proportion z-test (α 0.05, power 0.80), SRM χ² check, O’Brien–Fleming sequential boundary.
  • WCAG 2.2 Level AA: 1.4.3 contrast 4.5:1, 2.5.8 target size 24×24 CSS px, 2.4.11 focus not obscured, 3.3.8 accessible authentication.
  • Core Web Vitals “good” at p75: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1.
