    Methods & sources: the project data is simulated, the methods and thresholds are real
    • Double Diamond: UK Design Council (2005), “design the right thing, then design the thing right”, as taught by Nielsen Norman Group.
    • Stanford d.school modes and methods (Bootcamp Bootleg): beginner’s mindset, What? How? Why?, extreme users, empathy map, POV madlib, “How might we”, the seven brainstorm rules, post-it voting, Wizard of Oz prototyping.
    • Saturation around 12 interviews: Guest, Bunce & Johnson (2006). Survey margin of error at 95%: 1.96 × √(0.25 / n).
    • Think-aloud rounds of 5 users (Nielsen & Landauer). About 40 users for quantitative benchmarks (NN/g). SUS curved grades (Sauro & Lewis). Adjusted-Wald intervals.
    • HEART and Goals–Signals–Metrics: Google (2010). RICE: Intercom. Crazy 8s: Google Ventures design sprint.
    • A/B: two-proportion z-test, α 0.05, power 0.80, SRM χ² check. Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 Level AA.
