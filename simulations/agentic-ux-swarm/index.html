      Methods & sources: the project data is simulated, the methods and thresholds are real
      • Process: Double Diamond (Design Council 2005, NN/g) with Stanford d.school modes. An approval gate sits at the end of each phase.
      • Agents support research operations (screeners, scheduling, transcripts, tagging). Interviews and usability sessions are run with real participants and human moderators.
      • Synthetic users are treated as hypotheses rather than evidence: NN/g has found that AI-generated “users” skew positive and miss real behaviour.
      • Guardrails: PII redaction, verbatim-quote verification against transcripts (hallucination check), at least two sources per insight, reading-level and inclusive-language checks, fairness slices on outcomes.
      • Metrics: Nielsen’s 10 heuristics with 0–4 severity, WCAG 2.2 AA, SUS and SEQ, HEART, RICE, and an A/B test with α 0.05, power 0.80, an SRM check and an O’Brien–Fleming boundary.
